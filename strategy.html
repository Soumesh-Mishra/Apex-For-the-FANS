Know what the model can tell you.

Lap pace = base time + compound offset + degradation × tyre age − fuel gain × completed race laps. Each new stint starts on a fresh tyre with age zero. Safety-car laps add your slowdown, reduce tyre wear, and scale pit loss using your chosen percentages.

This lab does not model traffic, overtaking, tyre temperature, weather, red flags, driver differences or tyre-allocation/regulatory constraints. Its fastest pit laps are only the best choices inside this model. Scenario settings stay on this device.
