Your privacy at APEX

APEX is an independent Formula 1 fan dashboard. Google sign-in is optional; you can explore the dashboard without creating an APEX account.

Google sign-in

When enabled and used, Google provides APEX with your name, email address and stable Google account identifier. APEX verifies the credential on its server and uses this information to identify your signed-in session. APEX does not request your Google password, Gmail, Drive, contacts or calendar access.

Session cookies and preferences

APEX uses a Secure, HttpOnly session cookie that expires after seven days, and a temporary sign-in cookie that expires after ten minutes. Sign out of APEX to remove its session cookie. Your selected team is saved separately in this browser’s local storage. Reset to APEX Original or clear site data to remove that preference. Prediction leagues save your display name, account identifier, league memberships and predictions in the host’s database. They do not store your Google email address in that database. Guest play uses a separate Secure, HttpOnly cookie valid for up to 30 days; the server stores only a hash of its access token. Guest league access is lost when that cookie expires or is cleared. Signing in with Google in the same browser links those guest memberships to your account. Predictions are private until the qualifying deadline, then visible to league members.

Personal Paddock

Your team, spoiler preference, favourite drivers and saved races are stored on this device. On a connected server, Google sign-in can sync a separate account paddock. The server stores those preferences against your account identifier; it does not store your Google email address in this profile. Unsynced account changes are kept on the device for retry, separately for each account. Signing out restores the device paddock. Clearing site data removes device copies and pending edits, but does not delete settings already saved to the server. Spoiler hiding stays on locally until you explicitly turn it off.

Hosting and other services

Some deployments use a separate hosting sign-in gate. A standalone installation does not include that gate. Hosting access controls are separate from the optional APEX Google session. The dashboard loads racing data, images and fonts from the providers linked in its footer. Google’s own sign-in interface is delivered by Google.

Signing out

Signing out of APEX does not sign you out of Google or the hosting platform. You can manage APEX’s Google connection in your Google account’s third-party connections settings.
