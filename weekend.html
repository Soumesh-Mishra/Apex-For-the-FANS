THE STRATEGY BRIEFING

WHAT TO WATCH
01 · TYRES

Pace versus tyre life

Softer compounds can offer more grip, while degradation can make a longer stint slower. Watch lap-time trends as each stint develops.

02 · PIT WINDOW

Undercut or stay out?

An earlier stop can gain time on fresh tyres; staying out can preserve track position or benefit from a later interruption. Traffic matters.

03 · CONDITIONS

Expect the plan to change

Rain, safety cars and changing temperatures can alter a race. The weather panel is a daily forecast, not a session-specific prediction or a team strategy announcement.
